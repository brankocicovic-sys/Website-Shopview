FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is heavy-duty repair shop management software?

Heavy-duty repair shop management software helps diesel, truck, trailer, fleet, and equipment repair shops manage daily operations from one system. ShopView brings work orders, scheduling, technician time tracking, parts inventory, invoicing, customer records, and reporting together so shops can reduce paperwork and move repairs forward faster.

How does ShopView improve technician productivity?

ShopView gives technicians clear access to assigned jobs, repair notes, labor tracking, and job updates. Instead of waiting on paperwork or admin help, technicians can document work, log time, and move between jobs more efficiently.

Can ShopView manage parts inventory?

Yes. ShopView helps shops track parts usage, monitor inventory levels, manage ordering, and connect parts directly to repair orders. This helps reduce shortages, protect margins, and keep jobs from stalling.

Does ShopView support scheduling and preventive maintenance?

Yes. ShopView includes scheduling and workflow tools that help shops manage technician availability, job timelines, preventive maintenance, and repair progress in one place.
